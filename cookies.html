Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2025

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies Calculators.Finance uses, why we use them, and how you can manage your preferences. This policy supplements our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember preferences and collect usage information. We also use localStorage — a browser storage mechanism that works similarly to cookies but remains until explicitly cleared.

Essential cookies

The cookie_consent localStorage entry is strictly necessary to remember your consent decision. It is set as soon as you make a choice and cannot be disabled without affecting our ability to honour your preferences.

Analytics cookies (Google Analytics)

With your consent, Google Analytics 4 collects anonymised data about how visitors use the Site — which pages are most popular, how long visitors stay, and where they come from. This helps us improve the calculators. IP addresses are anonymised before storage. We do not use this data to identify individuals.

Google Analytics data is processed by Google LLC (US), covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising cookies (Google AdSense)

With your consent, Google AdSense displays advertisements on this Site. AdSense uses cookies to show ads relevant to your interests based on browsing history across websites that use Google advertising products. You can opt out at Google Ads Settings.

Managing your consent

Withdraw consent: To withdraw your consent at any time, open your browser's developer tools, go to Application → Local Storage → calculators.finance, and delete the cookie_consent entry. Refresh the page and the consent banner will reappear.

Block cookies in your browser: Most browsers allow you to block third-party cookies in settings. Note this may affect functionality on other sites.

Opt out of personalised ads: Visit adssettings.google.com or aboutads.info.

Reset your preference: — this will show the consent banner again on your next page load.

Do Not Track

Some browsers transmit a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. We currently do not alter our data collection practices in response to DNT signals, as there is no established standard for how websites should respond. Managing cookies as described above remains the most reliable way to limit tracking.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we add or remove cookies. The "last updated" date will reflect changes.
